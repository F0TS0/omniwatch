# Case data

Case data is declared and parsed locally in `data/cases.ts`. Signals contain their date, source label, constrained confidence movement, interpretation, preparation state, preventability, window state, and optional status transition.

The Luther sequence uses project-curated wording already present in the repository: sermons, correspondence, network visibility, threshold correlation, printing activity, and movement correlation. Its outcome is explicitly a counterfactual scenario.

Philadelphia 1776 and Estonia 1988 are archive shells. Their curation status is `PENDING`, so neither publishes detailed historical signals or an intervention outcome.

`data/present.ts` holds the illustrative Estonia 2026 sequence supplied for the product brief. It concerns an anonymous subject and only demonstrates a fictional connection of separate component systems.
