# OmniWatch V1 decisions

- V1 has no AI: every state follows visible local data, which keeps the demonstration deterministic and inspectable.
- Subjects are historical or anonymous: there is no live-person assessment, account system, or personal data collection.
- Browser profiling is removed: it is unrelated to the historical thought experiment and would violate the anonymous premise.
- Luther is the gold-standard case because it has the only complete project-curated signal sequence and the required confidence milestones.
- The label is **intervention confidence**, not a threat score: it describes a fictional simulation output.
- A fixed 70% threshold makes the status change legible and testable.
- The visitor chooses when to intervene to expose the tension between greater certainty and a narrower prevention window.
- Estonia 2026 follows a completed historical outcome so it remains a reveal, not a starting point for profiling.
- The existing face remains because it is visually distinctive and technically independent; all former product logic around it was removed.
